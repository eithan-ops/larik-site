The crowd is the screen — here's how

LARIK SHOW turns every phone in your crowd into one giant synced display: waves, pulses, colors and running text sweeping across hundreds of raised screens — all driven live from the DJ booth. No app to install, no signup, no LED wristbands to hand out.

From zero to glowing in under a minute

  1. The DJ starts a show at show.larik.ai — one tap, no account needed.
  2. The crowd scans a QR code shown on the venue screen (or printed on tickets). It opens in the browser — nothing to install.
  3. Every phone becomes a pixel. The system maps each phone's position in the crowd into one big grid.
  4. The DJ drives the light show live: waves, heartbeats, strobes, color sweeps, running text — or lets the autopilot sync everything to the beat of the music.
